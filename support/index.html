Support

Need help with Dwelly Sheets? Open a support request and include the property website, what you expected to happen, and any error message shown by the extension.

Open a support request

Before reporting a problem

  • Make sure you are on a supported property-detail page.
  • Confirm that you connected Google and created or selected a Dwelly Sheet.
  • Do not include private property notes, access tokens, passwords, or other sensitive information in a public support request.
